Your 5 fixes, in order

  1. Restart your Google reviews: text past customers a direct review link

    Start with customers from the last few years, then send it after every job. A handful of new reviews tells Google, and homeowners, that Walkers is active again. Keep replying to every one, as you already do.

    About 20 minutes to set up, then 2 minutes a job
    High impact
  2. Tidy the Google profile

    Remove the Painter and Painting categories, add your services (crack repair, interior drainage, sump pumps), and post a short "back up and running" update with a few recent job photos. Then switch Porch from General Contractor to Waterproofing.

    About 30 minutes
    High impact
  3. Put up a simple website on your own web address, and link it from the profile

    The UENI page reads like a template and shows a different phone number. A simple site of your own does more: "Basement Waterproofing in Columbus" in the headline, a call button that stays on screen as people scroll, a short contact form at the top, your 20 five-star reviews, your BBB listing, and a page for each main service (crack repair, interior drainage, sump pumps). Use one phone number on the site, Google, BBB and Yelp.

    About a week, start to finish
    High impact
  4. Get onto the lists AI quotes

    Start with Thumbtack and Angi, the two sources AI quoted most (one Angi profile also covers HomeAdvisor). Then the Entrepreneurs of Columbus top-rated list, TenBestFind, Biddaro and the 2026 "7 Best Basement and Foundation Experts in Columbus" roundup. Use exactly the same name, address and phone as Google.

    A few hours of submissions
    Medium impact
  5. Text back missed calls automatically

    When you're on a job and miss a call, the homeowner gets an instant text so they don't call the next company on the list.

    About an hour to set up
    Medium impact
